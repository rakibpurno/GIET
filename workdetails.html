Overview

GIET advances governance innovation and public service reform to enable transparent, accountable, and citizen-responsive institutions across Bangladesh. We partner with governments, development partners, and civil society to identify governance gaps, modernize service delivery frameworks, and align national systems with international best practices — fostering an environment grounded in integrity, accountability, and performance.

Combining expertise in public administration reform, regulatory modernization, and anti-corruption programming, and stakeholder engagement, GIET bridges the gap between policy design and real-world implementation — translating evidence and consultation into actionable, results-oriented reforms that outlast any individual project or intervention.

Our approach ensures that governance reform is technically robust, politically grounded, and institutionally owned — enabling effective collaboration between public agencies, civil society, and international partners at every stage of the reform cycle.

Through a participatory reform model, we promote continuous dialogue, leverage diagnostic evidence, and apply structured change management to shape reform priorities — ensuring institutions respond to real-world challenges while building long-term capacity for good governance.

Our Services Include

Designing and implementing service delivery standards and citizen charters across public institutions.

Improving grievance redress and citizen feedback mechanisms at national and local government level.

Conducting service process mapping and developing improvement plans with implementation support.

Strengthening institutional performance management, monitoring systems, and reporting frameworks.

Promoting ethics, integrity, anti-corruption awareness, and responsible conduct within institutions.

Building capacity of civil servants through certified governance, digital, and policy design training.

Facilitating multi-stakeholder consultations, national dialogues, and public-private engagement platforms.

Preparing policy briefs, diagnostic reports, and advocacy papers for government and development partners.

Supporting implementation of governance reform roadmaps with embedded advisory and change management.
