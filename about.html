About GIET

A non-partisan, non-profit think tank dedicated to advancing transparent governance, institutional reform, and economic transformation across Bangladesh.

Who We Are

Transforming Challenges
into Strategic
Opportunities

Through structured empirical investigations and systematic multi-sectoral stakeholder consultations, GIET directly addresses the systemic complexities surrounding public policy architecture and governance frameworks.

We deliver data-driven diagnostic blueprints that enable government agencies, international development institutions, and regulatory councils to design and execute structural changes.

Our Institution

Mission, Vision & Mandate

Mission

To advance transparent, accountable governance and sustainable economic transformation through rigorous research, policy advocacy, and implementation support.

Vision

A Bangladesh where institutions are transparent, economies are competitive, and citizens are empowered through evidence-based policy and good governance.

Mandate

A non-political, non-profit, voluntary and charitable organization operating across Bangladesh irrespective of nation, religion, caste, or creed.

Our Story

Built on a Belief that Better Governance is Possible

GIET was established by a group of policy practitioners, academics, and development professionals who witnessed firsthand the gap between policy design and real-world implementation in Bangladesh.

2021

Foundation Established

GIET was incorporated in Dhaka as a non-profit, non-governmental organization by founding members drawn from government, academia, and the development sector. The founding mandate focused on governance innovation and economic reform advisory.

2022

First Research Programme Launched

GIET published its inaugural set of policy briefs on trade facilitation and regulatory modernization, establishing its credentials as an evidence-based research institution. The organization convened its first national policy dialogue in Dhaka with 80+ participants from government and civil society.

2023

Expanded to 15 Focus Areas

The organization's mandate was broadened to encompass 15 thematic areas spanning governance, economic transformation, digital development, social inclusion, and climate sustainability — reflecting the complexity of Bangladesh's reform agenda.

2024

International Partnership Network

GIET formalized partnerships with international development organizations, bilateral agencies, and academic institutions — enabling joint research, technical advisory, and capacity-building programmes across South Asia.

Our Values

The Principles That Guide Our Work

Integrity

We uphold the highest standards of professional and intellectual honesty. Our research processes, partnerships, and governance practices reflect a commitment to transparency and accountability that we promote in others.

Independence

Our analysis is free from political influence, donor pressure, or institutional bias. We are funded by a diverse base of organizations and structured to protect the independence of our research conclusions at all times.

Inclusion

We believe that sustainable reform must include the voices of women, youth, persons with disabilities, and marginalized communities. Our programmes are designed to be equitable in both process and outcome.

Impact

We are a results-oriented organization. We define success not by publications produced or events held, but by measurable changes in policy, institutional practice, and the lives of citizens we ultimately serve.
