Our Experts

GIET's team of researchers, policy analysts, and practitioners brings decades of combined experience in governance reform, economic transformation, trade policy, and digital development across South Asia.

Leadership Team

GIET is led by a board of distinguished policy practitioners, economists, and institutional reform specialists committed to evidence-based governance transformation in Bangladesh.

CHAIRMAN

Dr. Aminur Rahman

PhD in Public Administration - University of Dhaka

Dr. Rahman brings over 25 years of experience in public administration reform, anti-corruption policy, and institutional capacity development. He previously served as Joint Secretary at the Cabinet Division and held senior advisory roles at UNDP Bangladesh. He chairs GIET's Board of Governors and oversees the organization's strategic direction and policy advocacy mandate.

Executive Director

Dr. Nusrat Jahan

PhD in Public Policy · London School of Economics

Dr. Jahan has over 18 years of experience in governance reform, trade facilitation, and economic policy across South Asia. She previously served as Senior Economist at the World Bank's Dhaka office and as Policy Advisor to the Ministry of Finance. As Executive Director, she leads GIET's research agenda, strategic partnerships, and operational management.

Vice Chairman

Mr. Tariqul Islam

MPA in Economic Policy · Harvard Kennedy School

Mr. Islam has 20 years of experience in trade policy and investment promotion in Bangladesh. He was Director General at BIDA and led the WTO Trade Facilitation Agreement task force. As Vice Chairman, he oversees GIET's economic transformation and trade facilitation.
