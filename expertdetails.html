Dr. Nusrat Jahan is the Executive Director of the Foundation for Governance Innovation and Economic Transformation (GIET), where she leads the organization's research agenda, external partnerships, and strategic development. She brings over 18 years of experience in governance reform, economic policy, and institutional development across Bangladesh and South Asia.

Prior to joining GIET, Dr. Jahan served as a Senior Economist at the World Bank's Dhaka Country Office, where she led economic analysis for Bangladesh's annual Country Economic Memorandum and provided technical assistance to the Ministry of Finance on macro-fiscal policy. Before her World Bank appointment, she worked as a Policy Advisor to the Cabinet Division of the Government of Bangladesh, supporting the design of the National Service Delivery Strategy.

Dr. Jahan's research focuses on the political economy of reform in developing democracies, with particular emphasis on trade facilitation, regulatory modernization, and the conditions under which governance reforms sustain beyond political transitions. She holds a PhD in Public Policy from the London School of Economics, an MPA from Princeton University's School of Public and International Affairs, and a BSS (Honours) in Economics from the University of Dhaka. She is a member of the Board of Governors of the Bangladesh Policy Research Institute and serves on the editorial board of the South Asian Journal of Governance.
