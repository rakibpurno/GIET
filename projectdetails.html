Strengthening Citizen–Centric Public Service Delivery in Bangladesh

Client: World Bank Group · Partner: Cabinet Division, Government of Bangladesh
Client
World Bank Group Asian Dev. Bank
Duration 18 Months
2024 – 2025
Location Bangladesh
Dhaka + 6 Districts
Sector
Public Administration Governance Reform
Status Completed First Quarter 2025

Project Overview

Bangladesh's public service delivery system has long been characterised by complex procedures, limited transparency, and insufficient mechanisms for citizens to provide feedback or seek redress when services fall short. GIET was engaged by the World Bank Group to conduct a comprehensive diagnostic of service delivery performance across six key ministries, and to produce an actionable reform roadmap with a fully costed 3-year implementation plan.

The project was implemented across seven locations — Dhaka and six districts — and combined quantitative service performance measurement with qualitative citizen experience research. A central feature was the deployment of a mystery shopper methodology to capture the actual citizen experience of accessing services, including informal facilitation costs and actual waiting times, rather than relying solely on official data.
